# Journal Sustainability — Financial Analysis

## Overview

This skill analyzes the financial health of a journal and identifies levers to improve profitability and long-term sustainability.

## When to Use

- Reviewing a journal's financial performance
- Analyzing APC yield and waiver impact
- Evaluating cost-per-decision efficiency
- Comparing journal economics across the portfolio
- Making pricing or volume adjustment recommendations

## Prerequisites

- `core-analytics` — article volumes, revenue data, cost allocations
- `core-salesforce` — APC pricing, discounts, waivers, invoicing data

## Workflow

1. **Revenue analysis** — Gross APC revenue, net revenue (after waivers/discounts), revenue per published article
2. **Cost analysis** — Editorial costs, per-article processing costs, marketing spend, technology allocation
3. **Profitability** — Contribution margin, fully-loaded margin, trend over 4 quarters
4. **Efficiency metrics** — Cost per decision, cost per published article, wasted effort (cost of rejected articles)
5. **Lever identification** — What moves the needle: volume, pricing, waiver policy, efficiency
6. **Scenario modeling** — What-if analysis for pricing changes, volume targets, waiver caps

## Output Format

- Financial summary dashboard
- Revenue waterfall (gross → net → contribution)
- Cost breakdown by category
- Profitability trend chart (quarterly)
- Lever analysis with estimated impact
- Scenario comparison table
