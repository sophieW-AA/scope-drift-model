# Partnerships — Institutional & Society Management

## Overview

This skill supports the management of Frontiers' institutional publishing agreements, national
consortia deals, and society publishing partnerships. It covers the full partnership lifecycle
from proposal to renewal.

## When to Use

- Preparing a partnership proposal for a new institution
- Modeling a flat-fee publishing agreement
- Analyzing usage and ROI for an existing partner
- Preparing for a renewal negotiation
- Comparing agreement types (APC discounts, read-and-publish, flat-fee)
- Generating partner usage reports
- Managing society publishing partnership discussions
- Tracking national consortia agreement status

## Prerequisites

- `core-salesforce` — partner records, agreement terms, invoicing, usage data
- `core-analytics` — publication volumes by institution, author data, article metrics
- `core-frontiers` — partnership program overview

## Partnership Types

### Institutional Agreements
- **APC discount agreements** — Partner institutions get reduced APCs
- **Read-and-publish** — Bundled reading + publishing access
- **Flat-fee agreements** — Fixed annual fee covering all publications from the institution
- **National consortia** — Country-level agreements (Austria, Finland, Luxembourg, Norway, Qatar, Switzerland, Sweden, UK)

### Society Publishing Partnerships
- Frontiers' platform and expertise available to learned societies
- Societies maintain editorial independence
- Frontiers provides technology, operations, and distribution
- Revenue-sharing models

## Workflow

### New Partnership Proposal
1. **Research the prospect** — Publication volume with Frontiers, total output, current publisher relationships
2. **Size the opportunity** — Estimate annual APC spend based on historical submissions
3. **Select agreement type** — Match to institution's needs and budget
4. **Model financials** — Revenue projection under different agreement structures
5. **Generate proposal** — Branded document with value proposition, terms, pricing
6. **Track pipeline** — Update Salesforce opportunity

### Renewal Preparation
1. **Usage report** — Articles published, APCs covered, waiver usage
2. **Impact report** — Citations, downloads, Altmetric mentions for partner's articles
3. **Benchmark** — Compare to similar institutions
4. **Identify growth** — New departments, emerging fields, upcoming research
5. **Renewal proposal** — Updated terms based on usage trends

### Flat-Fee Modeling
For flat-fee agreements, model:
- Historical publication volume (3-year trend)
- Projected growth rate
- Average APC (net of waivers/discounts)
- Break-even analysis (for Frontiers and for the partner)
- Scenario analysis: volume caps, overage provisions

## Output Format

- Partnership proposal document (branded)
- Usage & impact report (annual)
- Financial model (Excel with scenarios)
- Pipeline status dashboard
- Renewal recommendation brief
