# Article Marketing — Promotion & Impact

## Overview

This skill maximizes the visibility and real-world impact of individual published articles
through targeted promotion, media outreach, and social amplification.

## When to Use

- A high-impact article has been published and needs promotion
- Generating a press release for a notable study
- Creating social media posts for specific articles
- Tracking article-level impact (Altmetric, media mentions, social shares)
- Pitching a science story to journalists or media outlets
- Creating visual assets (social cards, infographics) for an article
- Identifying which articles to promote (selection criteria)

## Prerequisites

- `core-brand` — Frontiers visual identity for assets
- `core-analytics` — article metrics, download/citation data

## Article Selection Criteria

Not every article gets a promotion campaign. Prioritize articles that are:
- **High novelty** — First-of-its-kind findings, breakthrough methods
- **Broad appeal** — Relevant beyond the academic niche (health, environment, tech, society)
- **Timely** — Connected to current events, policy debates, or public interest
- **Visual** — Has compelling images, data visualizations, or video
- **Author-supported** — Authors willing to engage with media

## Promotion Workflow

### 1. Article Identification
- Monitor newly published articles for promotion candidates
- Check AIRA quality scores and early citation/download signals
- Consult with Journal Managers and editors for recommendations

### 2. Press Release
Structure:
- **Headline** — Newsworthy, jargon-free, active voice
- **Lede** — The key finding in one sentence a non-scientist can understand
- **Context** — Why this matters for society/health/environment
- **Key quotes** — From the lead author (request if not available)
- **Methods summary** — Brief, accessible description of how the study was done
- **Link** — DOI and open access link to full article
- **Media contact** — Frontiers press office

### 3. Social Media Assets
For each promoted article, generate:
- **Social card** — Key finding as a visual quote (branded, platform-sized)
- **Thread/carousel** — 3–5 slides summarizing the study for LinkedIn/Instagram
- **Short video script** — 30-second summary for TikTok/Reels (if applicable)

### 4. Media Outreach
- Identify relevant science journalists and outlets
- Tailor pitch to each outlet's audience
- Provide embargo-ready materials if pre-publication
- Follow up and track coverage

### 5. Impact Tracking
Track after promotion:
- Altmetric score and breakdown (news, social, policy, Wikipedia)
- Download spike vs. baseline
- Social media engagement (likes, shares, comments)
- Media pickups (news mentions)
- Author feedback

## Output Format

- Press release (ready for pressoffice.frontiersin.org)
- Social media posts (LinkedIn, X, Instagram — platform-specific)
- Social card visual brief (for design team or AI generation)
- Media pitch email draft
- Impact tracking report (7-day and 30-day post-promotion)
