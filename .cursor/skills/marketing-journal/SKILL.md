# Journal Marketing

## Overview

This skill supports marketing activities at the journal level — from launch campaigns to
ongoing author acquisition and visibility strategies.

## When to Use

- Planning a marketing campaign for a new or existing journal
- Creating social media posts about a journal or its content
- Drafting newsletter or email campaign copy
- Planning conference presence and sponsorship
- Improving journal landing page SEO
- Designing author acquisition strategies
- Generating marketing performance reports

## Prerequisites

- `core-brand` — Frontiers visual identity and tone
- `core-analytics` — journal traffic, submission sources, author demographics
- `core-salesforce` — campaign tracking, contact lists
- `core-email` — email template generation

## Marketing Channels

### Social Media
- **LinkedIn** — Primary channel for academic audiences. Share editorial highlights, new sections, editor spotlights.
- **X/Twitter** — Quick updates, article highlights, conference live-tweeting.
- **Facebook** — Community engagement, event promotion.
- **Instagram** — Visual content, behind-the-scenes, infographics.

### Email Campaigns
- New journal launch announcements
- Call for papers
- Research Topic promotion
- Impact Factor announcements
- Milestone celebrations (1000th article, etc.)

### Conference & Events
- Booth presence at key disciplinary conferences
- Editor networking events
- Sponsored sessions or workshops
- Promotional materials (flyers, banners, giveaways)

### SEO & Content
- Journal landing page optimization
- Aims & scope keyword alignment
- Blog posts and editorials
- Author testimonials and case studies

## Campaign Templates

### New Journal Launch
1. Pre-launch teaser (4 weeks before) — social + email
2. Launch announcement — press release + social + email blast
3. Call for papers — targeted email to potential authors in the field
4. Editor spotlight series — profiles of Chief Editors on social
5. First articles celebration — when first papers are published

### Impact Factor Announcement
1. Congratulations post (social + email to board + email to authors)
2. Infographic with key metrics
3. Press release (if significant milestone)
4. Update journal landing page

### Submission Drive
1. Identify target author segments (from BigQuery)
2. Personalized outreach emails
3. Research Topic promotion
4. Social campaign highlighting recent high-impact articles

## Output Format

- Campaign brief (objectives, audience, channels, timeline, budget)
- Social media post drafts (platform-specific)
- Email copy (subject line + body)
- SEO recommendations (keywords, meta descriptions)
- Campaign performance report template
