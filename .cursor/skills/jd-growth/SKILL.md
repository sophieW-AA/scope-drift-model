# Journal Growth — Submission Playbook

## Overview

This skill analyzes the submission funnel and recommends actions to grow a journal's submission volume and quality.

## When to Use

- A journal's submissions are flat or declining
- Need to analyze the submission-to-publication funnel
- Planning Research Topics to drive submissions
- Identifying which article types perform best
- Designing author outreach or marketing campaigns

## Prerequisites

- `core-analytics` — submission funnel data, author demographics, article performance
- `core-salesforce` — campaign history, author contacts, Research Topic data
- `core-openalex` — field growth trends, competing journal volumes

## Workflow

1. **Funnel analysis** — Map submissions → desk decisions → review → acceptance → publication
2. **Trend decomposition** — Isolate growth drivers (organic, Research Topics, campaigns, geographic)
3. **Article type analysis** — Which types (original research, review, methods) drive volume and citations
4. **Research Topic planning** — Identify trending subfields, propose RT titles, estimate submission yield
5. **Author pool analysis** — Who submits, who doesn't, resubmission rates, geographic opportunities
6. **Campaign recommendations** — Targeted outreach suggestions with expected ROI
7. **Growth forecast** — 12-month submission projection under different scenarios

## Output Format

- Funnel dashboard with conversion rates at each stage
- Growth driver decomposition chart
- Research Topic proposals (3–5) with rationale
- Author outreach plan
- 12-month growth forecast (base / stretch / conservative)
