# Frontiers Media SA — Company Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

**Frontiers Media SA** is one of the world's largest and most impactful open-access research publishers.
Headquartered in **Lausanne, Switzerland**, it operates a gold open-access publishing model across
222 community-driven journals spanning approximately 1,700 academic specialty sections.

- **Website:** https://www.frontiersin.org
- **Founded:** 2007
- **Headquarters:** Lausanne, Switzerland (see Office Locations below for all offices)
- **Legal entity:** Frontiers Media SA (Swiss corporation)
- **CEO & Co-founder:** Dr. Kamila Markram
- **Co-founder & Board Chairperson:** Prof. Henry Markram

## Mission & Values

**Mission:** Make science open — so that scientists can collaborate better and innovate faster to
deliver the solutions that enable healthy lives on a healthy planet.

**Five core values:**

1. **Researcher-centricity** — Place the researcher at the center of everything
2. **Collaboration** — Build communities and work together
3. **Innovation** — Be bold, driven by technology and AI
4. **Quality** — Rigorous standards in everything
5. **Speed** — Work efficiently and decisively

## Publishing Model

### Gold Open Access
All articles are immediately and permanently free to read under **Creative Commons CC-BY 4.0**.
Revenue comes from **Article Processing Charges (APCs)** paid upon acceptance.

Frontiers is a signatory of **DORA** (San Francisco Declaration on Research Assessment) and supports
responsible use of metrics as outlined in the Leiden Manifesto and the Metric Tide.

### Journal Structure
Frontiers uses a unique three-tiered journal structure:

1. **Field journals** — The top-level journal (e.g., "Frontiers in Neuroscience")
2. **Specialty sections** — Independent editorial units within each field journal, each with its own editorial board
3. **Cross-listing** — The same specialty section can appear in multiple journals to support interdisciplinary research

This structure enables journals to evolve as fields advance. Field Chief Editors can launch new sections,
scale editorial boards, and maintain rigorous peer review even as journals grow.

### Peer Review Model
Frontiers uses a **collaborative, single-anonymized peer review**:

1. **Pre-review quality checks** — AIRA (AI) + research integrity team screen every manuscript (~32% of manuscripts blocked/rejected before peer review)
2. **Independent review** — At least 2 reviewers evaluate independently using structured questionnaires
3. **Interactive review** — Authors and reviewers discuss in an online forum, mediated by the Associate Editor
4. **Decision** — Associate Editor accepts only if all reviewers endorse; Chief Editor can reject
5. **Transparency** — Reviewer and editor names are published on accepted articles

**Key safeguard:** Only independent academic Associate Editors (not Frontiers staff) can accept manuscripts.
No one has a financial incentive to accept articles. Chief editors receive an honorarium based on submission
levels, not acceptances.

### Editorial Independence Principles

Frontiers follows COPE and WAME guidelines on editorial independence:

- **Editor authority** — Editors hold full authority over scientific content decisions before publication
- **Transparency & accountability** — Clear policies guide operations; publisher involvement in ethical issues is transparent
- **Safeguarding research integrity** — Corrective measures (rejection, correction, retraction) for confirmed integrity breaches; scientific judgments remain editors' sole responsibility
- **Integrity & independence** — These principles protect editorial independence while ensuring reliability of the scientific record

## Research Integrity

Frontiers has a 66-person research integrity department led by **Elena Vicario** (Director of Research Integrity),
supported by 14 research integrity and auditing managers.

### Three-Layered Approach

1. **Before publication:** Extensive integrity and quality checks at initial validation — team blocks and rejects ~32% of manuscripts before peer review
2. **Identifying fraud:** Dedicated auditing division (created 2022) proactively investigates patterns of misconduct, uncovers large-scale organizational fraud networks (papermills)
3. **Post-publication:** Handles all allegations of misconduct; monitors Retraction Watch, PubPeer, social media; follows COPE guidelines

### AIRA — AI Review Assistant (since 2018)
AI system that performs 40+ automated quality checks on every manuscript before peer review:
- Language quality assessment
- Image integrity / manipulation detection
- Plagiarism screening
- Conflict of interest detection
- Scope classification (recommends best section/journal)
- Fabricated content detection
- Integration with industry-leading fraud screening tools

AIRA was purpose-built for academic publishing, developed completely in-house, and continuously updated.
It supports — never replaces — expert human judgment.

## Key Products & Innovations

### Research Topics
Themed, collaborative article collections organized by leading researchers around emerging areas.
Researchers propose topics, invite contributors, and curate interdisciplinary collections across journals.

### FAIR2 Data Management (available from October 2025)
AI-powered data management service for research. Uses AI to curate and structure data to be
Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable — making datasets AI-ready, citable, and reusable.

**Key features:**
- **FAIR2 Data Article** — New article type focused exclusively on a single dataset (not hypothesis-driven)
- **Interactive FAIR2 Data Portal** — Custom-tailored with AI chat, visualizations, and notebooks for reuse
- **AI data steward "Clara"** — Assists with metadata and initial preparation
- **Peer-reviewed and citable** — Datasets get the same recognition as traditional research articles
- **Pricing:** CHF 5,500 (up to 50 GB) — includes curation, certification, publication, portal hosting, lifetime preservation
- **License:** Published datasets under ODC-BY 1.0
- **Compliance:** Aligned with NIH, Horizon Europe, UKRI funder mandates

FAIR2 extends traditional FAIR principles with AI-readiness, ethical governance, and a structured, open specification.

### Frontiers for Young Minds
Free open-access science journal where young people (kids) review articles written by leading scientists,
ensuring research is accessible to younger audiences. Includes the Nobel Collection (33+ Nobel Laureates
across four volumes). In March 2026, launched the **SDG Collection** video series — a collaboration with
**KAUST** (King Abdullah University of Science and Technology) and **UNDP-Saudi Arabia** where young minds
interview leading scientists about how research contributes to the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

### Frontiers Planet Prize
Annual global prize celebrating researchers whose science offers scalable solutions
to protect planetary health:
- **Three prizes of USD $1 million each** awarded annually (USD $3 million total)
- Focus on breakthrough research advancing **planetary boundary science** — the nine limits defining Earth's health
- Scientists enter through their institution/university acting as National Nominating Bodies (NNBs)
- First international champions awarded in 2023; second edition in 2024; 2025 International Champion: Prof Arunima Malik (University of Sydney)
- Planetary boundary framework brought to COP30 for the first time through the Planetary Science Pavilion

### Frontiers in Science
Flagship multidisciplinary journal (launched 2023) focused on transformational science to accelerate
solutions for healthy lives on a healthy planet. Features **Deep Dive** sessions — live and recorded
video events bringing researchers, policy experts, and innovators together to discuss lead articles
(e.g., plastic pollution & climate change, obesity & climate change, food safety & sustainability).
Also produces **article hubs** — curated pages grouping the lead article with expert commentary.

### Frontiers Science House
High-energy hub pioneered by Frontiers that brings science to the center of global dialogue and action.
Launched at the World Economic Forum Annual Meeting 2026 in Davos, located on the Davos Promenade.
50+ sessions, 168 speakers, 1,200+ attendees, in partnership with 50+ global organizations.
Notable speakers included Jimmy Wales (Wikipedia), Aloe Blacc, Josef Aschbacher (ESA Director General),
Johan Rockström, Henry Markram, and Kamila Markram. Key announcements included the JEDI Draghi Tracker,
SPARK Microgravity orbital cancer lab, AI Polis urban planning platform, and the world's first live
demonstration of structural battery technology. Also produced the **"Overheard at Frontiers Science House"**
short-form video series covering themes like AI & healthcare and the energy revolution.

### Frontiers Policy Labs
Initiative connecting experts from business, science, and policy to strengthen the dialogue between
scientific research and informed policymaking.

### Frontiers Forum
Annual event (since 2015) bringing together researchers, policymakers, and innovators.
First hybrid event in 2023 with keynotes from Prof Johan Rockstrom, Ban Ki-Moon, and Jane Goodall.

### Science Unlocked
Documentary hosted by CNBC spotlighting Frontiers' mission to make science open and its role in
accelerating breakthroughs across health, climate resilience, technology, the economy, and society.

### Frontiers Discover
Platform (launched 2022) allowing community reviewers more control over the research they choose to review.
Reviewers can browse submitted manuscripts, identify submissions matching their expertise, and volunteer to review.

### Publishing Partnerships
Frontiers' platform and expertise made available to learned societies and institutions,
enabling their transition to open access. Published via frontierspartnerships.org.

### Institutional Partnerships
Open access publishing agreements with 740+ institutions (2025 AR figure). National consortia agreements with
Austria, Finland, Luxembourg, Norway, Qatar, Switzerland, Sweden, UK, and others.
**Flat fee open access model** rolled out in 2024 — institutions pay one annual fee covering all their
researchers' OA publishing, simplifying institutional support. Piloted in 2023 with partners in Europe
and North America.

### OA Switchboard Integration (March 2026)
Frontiers integrated with the OA Switchboard to automate metadata exchange between publishers,
institutions, libraries, and funders — streamlining open access workflows and reporting.

### World Economic Forum Collaboration
Co-produced "Top 10 Emerging Technologies" reports with WEF in 2023, 2024, and 2025.
At Davos 2026, demonstrated structural batteries (a 2025 Top 10 technology) for the first time live.

### UNESCO Science Decade Collaboration
Collaboration with UNESCO's International Decade of Sciences for Sustainable Development (2024–2033).
First Science Decade Global Conference planned for Paris, July 2026.

### Open Science Charter
Launched at COP28 (2023), calling for all publicly funded research to be fully open by 2030.
