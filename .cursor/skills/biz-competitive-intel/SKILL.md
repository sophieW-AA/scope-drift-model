# Competitive Intelligence — Publishing Landscape

## Overview

This skill provides competitive context about the scholarly publishing industry, helping Frontiers
make informed strategic decisions about journal development, pricing, and positioning.

## When to Use

- Analyzing a competitor's journal portfolio in a specific field
- Comparing APC pricing across publishers
- Tracking new journal launches by competitors
- Assessing market share trends in open access
- Understanding industry-wide challenges (paper mills, predatory publishing, AI policies)
- Providing competitive context for `jd-research` and `jd-strategy` analyses
- Benchmarking Frontiers' performance against the industry

## Key Competitors

### Major Open Access Publishers
| Publisher | Profile |
|-----------|---------|
| **MDPI** | Large OA publisher, Basel. High volume, rapid growth. ~420 journals. Often compared to Frontiers. |
| **PLOS** | Pioneer OA publisher (PLOS ONE). Non-profit. Smaller but influential. |
| **Hindawi** (Wiley) | Was independent OA publisher, acquired by Wiley 2021, journals being wound down/merged. |
| **BioMed Central** (Springer Nature) | Early OA publisher, now part of Springer Nature. ~300 journals. |

### Traditional Publishers with OA Programs
| Publisher | Profile |
|-----------|---------|
| **Springer Nature** | Largest by revenue. Nature + Springer + BMC. Hybrid and gold OA. Holtzbrinck is investor in both SN and Frontiers. |
| **Elsevier** | Largest by articles. Scopus owner. Expanding OA via transformative agreements. |
| **Wiley** | Major hybrid publisher. Acquired Hindawi. Significant OA portfolio. |
| **Taylor & Francis** | Part of Informa. Growing OA presence. |
| **SAGE** | Mid-size, strong in social sciences. |
| **IEEE** | Dominant in engineering/computing. Mostly subscription with OA options. |

### Competitive Dimensions

| Dimension | What to compare |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Volume** | Articles/year, journals, sections |
| **Pricing** | APC levels, waivers, discounts, flat-fee deals |
| **Speed** | Time-to-first-decision, time-to-publication |
| **Quality signals** | Impact Factors, CiteScores, indexing coverage |
| **Innovation** | AI tools, open data, preprints integration |
| **Reach** | Geographic author distribution, institutional agreements |
| **Integrity** | Retraction rates, COPE membership, fraud detection |

## Data Sources

| Source | What we get |
|--------|-------------|
| OpenAlex | Publication volumes by publisher, journal, field |
| Crossref | DOI registrations, publisher output trends |
| Scopus/WoS | Impact metrics, indexing status |
| Publisher websites | APC pricing, journal lists, policies |
| Industry reports | STM Association, DOAJ, OASPA data |
| News | Retraction Watch, Scholarly Kitchen, publisher press releases |

## Common Analyses

### Field-Level Competitive Landscape
For a specific research field:
1. Identify all active journals (by publisher) using OpenAlex concepts
2. Rank by volume, citations, Impact Factor
3. Map APC pricing
4. Identify gaps Frontiers could fill

### APC Benchmarking
Compare pricing for similar journals:
- Frontiers APC vs. competitor APCs in same field
- Waiver/discount policies
- Flat-fee vs. per-article pricing trends

### Market Share Tracking
Using OpenAlex/Crossref data:
- Frontiers share of OA articles by field
- Year-over-year trend
- Growth rate vs. competitors

## Output Format

- Competitor profile briefing
- Field-level competitive landscape matrix
- APC benchmarking table
- Market share trend charts
- Strategic implications summary
