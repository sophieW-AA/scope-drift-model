About this network. Built from run 20261016_150236 of the AIRA author citation network. Frontiers journals onlyPublished 2023–2026817,257 authors173,075 papers · 207 journals9.2M author links What this does and does not include ▾
  • Papers: only articles in the run's 215 journal IDs. In AIRA these resolve to 209 Frontiers titles, 2 Frontiers Partnerships titles (Advances in Drug and Alcohol Research, Journal of Abdominal Wall Surgery) and one unexpected title (The Female Patient, about 170 papers, likely an ID mismatch); 3 IDs did not resolve. 207 journals have papers in the window. Papers from other publishers are not in the network.
  • Authors: anyone who wrote at least one of those papers. Paper counts and lists cover only their 2023–2026 Frontiers papers, not their full career.
  • Links: two authors are linked when one's paper cites the other's and both papers are in this set. Citations to or from other publishers, or before 2023, are ignored. A link is citation-based, not coauthorship; teal lines mark pairs who are also coauthors.
  • Weights: first and last authors count 1.0, middle authors 0.25. Older citations count for less (temporal decay, τ = 5 years). Link counts depend on how often a person's papers are cited, not on how many they wrote.
  • Communities: Leiden clustering of the author network into 27 macro, 250 meso and 777 micro communities. They have no topic labels; the top journal is shown instead.
  • Community map: positions come only from citation between macro communities. Use the lines, not the gaps, to judge how related two groups are.
  • Details: __NEMB__ authors (anyone in the top 40 of their micro, meso or macro community) have a details page with their 50 strongest links. Other people appear greyed out and cannot be opened.
